Auto-refreshed every 3 hours between 9am and 9pm while the Claude app is open. Built from two PakWheels searches — 600–660cc and 670–1350cc — plus model keyword searches on OLX, then filtered to a fixed list of Japanese models and location-verified against the area on each ad. Commercial vans (Hijet, Every, Carry, Scrum, Minicab, Clipper, Acty, Vamos, Town Box, Sambar, Dias, Atrai) are excluded, as are Mehran, Bolan, Ravi, Cuore, Charade and Kix. Colour and registration city are read from each PakWheels ad's own page and cached; OLX listing cards carry neither, so those two fields are blank on OLX rows — open the ad to check. "Reg" is where the car is registered, not where it is parked. A Punjab- or Lahore-registered car being sold in Islamabad means transfer paperwork and cost. OLX fields come from seller-written text, so variant and mileage are only as reliable as what was typed. Pakistani-assembled Suzuki Wagon R is 1000cc — OLX Wagon Rs were only kept where the ad said Stingray, FX, FZ or Hybrid, so some genuine JDM 660cc cars may be missing. Always get an auction sheet verification before paying for a JDM import.
